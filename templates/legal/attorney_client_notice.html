Attorney–Client Notice

Last updated: [DATE — set on approval]

LexImmigrate is a service of a real law firm, and that comes with real rules about when you are — and are not — our client. This notice explains it plainly, because the difference matters for your rights.

1. Using this website does not make you our client

Browsing this site, reading our guides, completing the free eligibility check, or sending us your contact information does not create an attorney–client relationship with YSL Immigration Lawyers. The eligibility check is an automated estimate based on your answers — it is general information about the law's requirements, not legal advice about your situation.

3. The scope of representation depends on your package

Texas permits attorneys to provide limited-scope representation — handling defined parts of a matter rather than all of it — with the client's informed consent. That is how our packages work:

  • DIY — you prepare and file your own application. The Firm provides the guided process and a set amount of attorney consultation time. The Firm does not review your completed application or represent you before USCIS.
  • Enhanced — you prepare and file your own application, and the Firm additionally reviews your full application and evidence before you file and helps you prepare for your interview. The Firm does not file for you or appear with you.
  • Full Service — the Firm represents you fully: preparing, filing, and tracking your application, and appearing with you at your USCIS interview.

Your engagement agreement will state your package's exact scope. Anything outside that scope is not part of the representation unless you and the Firm agree to it in writing.

4. Confidentiality — before and after you become a client

We treat everything you submit through this site as confidential and handle it as described in our Privacy Policy. But you should know the legal distinction:

  • Before an attorney–client relationship exists, information you send us may not be protected by attorney–client privilege. Please don't send detailed case documents until we've asked for them through your client portal.
  • After the relationship begins, what you share with the Firm in the course of the representation is protected by the attorney's professional duty of confidentiality and, where it applies, attorney–client privilege.

5. No guarantee of results

Past results — including the experiences described in testimonials on this site — do not guarantee the outcome of your case. Every immigration matter depends on its own facts, and decisions rest with the U.S. government.
