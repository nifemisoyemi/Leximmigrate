About LexImmigrate

What is LexImmigrate?

LexImmigrate is a service that puts you in control of your U.S. citizenship application. It isn't a law firm by itself, and it isn't a forms website either — it's a guided platform backed by licensed immigration lawyers, where you decide how much attorney time you want, from doing it yourself to having an attorney handle everything.

How does it work?

Three steps. First, check your eligibility — a few plain-language questions, free, about two minutes. Second, see where you stand and which package we recommend for your situation. Third, choose your package, create your account, and start your guided process — with as much or as little attorney support as you want.

Is the eligibility check legal advice?

No — think of it like checking the government's own requirements, just in plain language. It tells you whether you likely meet the criteria for naturalization. Legal advice about your specific situation comes from the licensed attorney time included in every package.

Packages & pricing

What do I get with each package?

Every package includes the full guided process — step-by-step instructions, the latest official forms, your document checklist, and time with a licensed immigration attorney. DIY is for people who want to run the whole thing themselves, with attorney time available whenever they choose to use it. Enhanced adds a complete review: an attorney checks your entire application and evidence before you file, and prepares you for your interview. Full Service means an attorney prepares and files everything for you and stands with you at your USCIS interview — we take care of it, start to finish.

Which package is right for me?

The eligibility check recommends one based on your situation. But you're free to choose any package you want. And if you're not sure, don't guess: tell us, and someone will get in touch to talk it through before you buy. Packages are non-refundable, so we'd rather you choose with confidence than choose fast.

What happens if my package doesn't fit my needs?

Packages are one-time purchases and non-refundable — so the right move is to make sure before you buy. The free eligibility check points you to the right level, and if you're on the fence, someone will talk it through with you first. If your needs grow partway through — say you started DIY and decide you want an attorney to review everything after all — you can purchase additional support separately at any time.

Why one flat price instead of hourly billing?

Traditional legal help usually means a retainer up front and bills by the hour — and no way to know what the total will be. LexImmigrate is flat-fee: one price, known before you start, no surprise invoices. You choose the level of help; the price doesn't move.

Are packages refundable?

No — all packages are one-time purchases and non-refundable. That's exactly why the eligibility check is free, why we recommend a package instead of guessing, and why you're able to consult with someone before you buy if you're unsure.

Does the price include government filing fees?

No. The government charges its own filing fees, which you pay directly to USCIS — no provider can include or control them. Your guided process shows you the current official fees and exactly when and how to pay them, so there are no surprises.

Good to know

Is it true that having an attorney will help my case?

Good representation helps — it limits your exposure to mistakes, missed evidence, and avoidable delays. But the decision is made by the government, not by any attorney. That's exactly why LexImmigrate works the way it does — the platform is designed around your needs, so you get the level of attorney involvement that fits your case.

Do you guarantee my application will be approved?

No — and you should be cautious of anyone who does. Only the government decides applications. What we promise is a clear process, complete and well-prepared paperwork, and the attorney support you chose.

How long does the process take?

That's up to the government — processing times vary by field office and can run from several months to over a year. What you control is submitting a complete, well-documented application the first time, which avoids the delays that are avoidable. That's what the guided process is built for.

Can I use LexImmigrate from anywhere?

Yes. U.S. immigration is federal, so it works the same no matter which state you live in. The whole process happens online, on your schedule.
