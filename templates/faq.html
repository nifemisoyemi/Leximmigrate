Getting started

Is this a real law firm?

Yes. LexImmigrate is a service of YSL Immigration Lawyers, founded by attorney Yohana Saucedo, with offices in Austin and Laredo, Texas. It is not a forms website — the guidance, reviews, and representation in our packages come from licensed immigration attorneys.

Is the eligibility check really free?

Yes — free, about two minutes, and no account or payment needed. You answer plain-language questions about the legal requirements for naturalization, and we tell you where you likely stand and which package fits your situation.

What happens after I finish the eligibility check?

You see your result right away: whether you're likely eligible, and the level of support we recommend. If you want to move forward, you create an account, choose your package, and begin the guided process. If you're not ready, nothing happens — no obligation.

What if the check says I'm not eligible yet?

"Not yet" is often just "not yet" — for example, you may simply need to wait until you're within 90 days of your 3- or 5-year mark as a permanent resident. We'll tell you the reason, and you can book a consultation so an attorney can confirm your timeline or look at options the questionnaire can't see.

The check is automated — is it legal advice?

No. It's an estimate based on your answers and current USCIS requirements. A licensed attorney confirms your eligibility before anything is filed, and our Attorney–Client Notice explains exactly when you become a client of the firm.

Packages & pricing

What's the difference between the three packages?

All three include the full guided process, the latest official forms, a document checklist, and attorney time. The difference is how much of the work the firm does. DIY: you prepare and file everything yourself, with 60 minutes of attorney consultation to use when you need it. Enhanced: you still file, but an attorney reviews your complete application and evidence first and preps you for the interview. Full Service: the firm prepares and files for you, and an attorney is with you at your USCIS interview.

The process

How long does naturalization take?

It varies by USCIS field office and caseload — commonly several months to over a year from filing to oath. We can't speed up the government, but we can make sure your application is complete and well-documented, which avoids the delays you can control. USCIS publishes current processing times on its website, and your guided process links to them.

Do you guarantee my application will be approved?

No — and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. Only the U.S. government decides applications. What we promise is diligent, competent work at the level of the package you choose.
